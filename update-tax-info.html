Update Employee Tax Info

For an already-hired employee's W-4 — a correction to what's on file, or a genuine new W-4 from a life change.

What's this update for?

Correction
Fixing a typo or mistake in what's already on file — an address, a spelling error, a number entered wrong. This does not change the original signing date.
New W-4 — Life Change
The employee got married, had a child, or otherwise wants to change their actual withholding. This is a genuinely new signed W-4 — the old one is kept on file, and this one gets today's date.
